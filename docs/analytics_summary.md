**Analytics Summary**

Sky Vision evaluates ball-centric decision quality using the PER-10 framework, quantifying how well receivers and defensive backs anticipate, separate, execute, track, and improvise around the ball. The analytics in Phase A confirm that PER-10 captures meaningful performance variation and identifies a quantifiable skill dimension related to ball outcomes.

**1\. PER-10 Correlates With Key Offensive Performance Metrics**

Higher offensive PER10 scores were associated with better on-field results: - Yards Gained: Receivers with higher PER10 scores tended to gain more yards. - EPA: Higher PER10 produced slightly positive Expected Points Added. - Completion Probability: A clear positive slope indicated that seeing and solving the rep increased catch likelihood.

**2\. Defensive PER-10 Suppresses Offensive Outcomes**

Defensive analytics showed the inverse: - Higher DB PER10 scores corresponded to fewer yards allowed, - Lower offensive EPA,

Conclusion: PER10 successfully differentiates execution quality for both sides of the rep. **3\. Quick Instinct Archetype Is Statistically Distinct**

Players classified as Quick Instinct (QI) \[Top 25% of Eyes and Innovation or Improv Score\] demonstrated superior PER10 performance: - QI mean PER10: 6.47 - Other mean PER10: 5.44 - t-statistic: 6.3254 - p-value: 0.0 - Cohen's d: 1.447 (very large effect)

Conclusion: Quick Instinct players are quantitatively better at ball interaction and decision execution. **4\. Predictive Modeling**

Model Results: Accuracy: 0.533 AUC: 0.575

Feature Importance Ranking: A (Anticipation) was the strongest signal, validating the framework design.

**Core Insight**

Ball outcomes are shaped by perceptual speed and adaptive intelligence-not athleticism alone.

This confirms that PER-10 and Quick Instinct provide a scalable lexicon for ball-centric performance analysis.
